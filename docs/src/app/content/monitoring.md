## Start monitoring

Open [the Monitoring page](https://themys.ca/monitoring) and enter the direct
URL of a public Terms of Service, Terms of Use, or Privacy Policy document.
Themys identifies the document type, stores a baseline, and checks it on the
frequency you choose.

## Plan limits and alerts

| Plan | Active documents | Email alerts |
|------|------------------|--------------|
| **Free** | 1 | Dashboard alerts only |
| **Personal** | 10 | Yes |
| **Founder** | 25 | Yes |

The limit applies to active monitored documents. A change creates an alert in
the app. Personal and Founder plans can also receive one email per alert through
the verified Themys email service.

Each change alert is backed by the same analysis and structured extraction used
by a regular scan. Select **View full scan** on an alert to open the complete
clauses, risk analysis, compliance information, and extraction result.

## Disable notifications or monitoring

Open **Alerts** to read document changes. Choose **Mark as read** on an alert to
clear its unread status; the alert remains available for reference.

Use **Email on / Email off** beside a monitored document to change its email
preference. Select **Remove** to stop future checks and alerts entirely. Email
delivery is authenticated and retried when a temporary delivery failure occurs;
the same alert is never intentionally sent twice.

Monitoring works only with public documents that Themys can identify with high
confidence. If a URL changes document type or becomes inaccessible, the
subscription is marked for review instead of generating a misleading alert.
