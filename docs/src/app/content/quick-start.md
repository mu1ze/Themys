## What is Themys?

Themys automatically analyzes Terms of Service and privacy policies, flagging clauses worth reviewing and assigning an attention level so you know where to focus.

## Current workflow: the web app

The Themys web app is the currently supported way to scan documents. The Chrome
extension is coming soon and is not available in the Chrome Web Store yet.

## Create Your Account

1. Go to [themys.ca](https://themys.ca) and click **Sign Up**
2. Create a free account or sign in
3. The free tier includes 15 reviews per month, one contract paste/PDF trial, and no credit card.

## Your First Scan

### With the Web App

1. Go to [themys.ca/scan/new](https://themys.ca/scan/new)
2. Enter the URL of any public Terms of Service or Privacy Policy page
3. Click **Scan Now** and wait for results

## Understanding Your Results

The results page opens with a summary and offers these views in its navigation:

| View | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Summary** | Attention level, finding counts, and priority findings |
| **Flagged** | Clauses worth reviewing, with explanations and supporting wording |
| **Actionable Insights** | Concrete steps and requests you can start now |
| **Compliance** | Automated framework connections when available |
| **Extraction** | Generated section summaries when available |
| **Full Document** | The stored original text for checking context |

Open a finding to read more. Source links take you to the **Full Document** view and attempt to highlight matching wording.

### Attention Levels

| Label | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| Standard | Typical clauses with lower review priority |
| Worth reviewing | Some clauses are worth reading before agreeing |
| Needs attention | Clauses deserve careful review before accepting |

## Your Dashboard

The dashboard keeps your open reviews and watched services together:

- **Start a review** by choosing **Scan a website**, **Paste your text**, or **Upload a PDF**. Your plan's existing review limits still apply.
- **Pick up where you left off** shows recent scans with an open decision. Search this list by document or service name, or open **History** for older scans.
- **On your radar** shows watched services, with unread alerts first. Choose **Manage** to read alerts or change monitoring settings.
- **Worth a conversation** brings together suggested asks from recent reviews. Expand additional asks or open the original results for context.
